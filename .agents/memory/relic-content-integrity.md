---
name: Relic content integrity
description: Content and trust requirements for the Relic Photography website.
---

For the Relic Photography website, do not invent awards, years of experience, wedding counts, client identities, testimonials, prices, package inclusions, certifications, team members, equipment, facilities, ratings, guarantees, or social URLs. Clearly identify temporary imagery and review copy as placeholders, not genuine portfolio work or customer feedback. The enquiry form must hand off to WhatsApp and must not claim an email was sent.

**Why:** the user explicitly required that trust be earned through the design and supplied information, not fabricated claims.

**How to apply:** preserve these constraints when updating website copy, imagery, reviews, package details, or contact flows.
